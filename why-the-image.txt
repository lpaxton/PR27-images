1. Breakthroughs

Mushroom: The mushroom above ground is a small part of a large network under the soil. A breakthrough is the visible tip of a lot of hidden groundwork.
Seedling through asphalt: The seedling grows where a crack, some moss and the warmth gave it a chance. Breakthroughs come more easily when the conditions are right.

2. Value of Effort

Worn steps: Each tread is dished in the middle from decades of feet. Effort leaves a visible mark over time.
Stairs beside an escalator: Both lead to the same bright exit, but one takes work and one doesn’t. Same destination, different effort.

3. Future of Financial Advice: woodland trail
There’s a marked main path with paint blazes and a worn shortcut beside it, so people choose between guided and self-directed routes. That matches advice ranging from full support to doing it yourself.

4. How We Play / Pleasure: melting soft serve
A cheap, brief treat on a park bench. Pleasure often comes from small, frequent moments like this, not big events.

5. Small Businesses: half-raised shop shutter at dawn
Someone is opening for the day, with warm light spilling onto the street. It’s the daily act of running a small business.

6. Revisits: sun-faded snapshots on a windowsill
Old prints that have faded until you can’t make them out, like looking back at earlier predictions. You remember having them, but the details have washed away.

7. Memory & Context: library card catalog
Drawers half open, each one an index into a much larger store, like memory organized and kept at scale. It also echoes the old way of finding context before search.
